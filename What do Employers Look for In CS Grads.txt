♫ Soft Skills
	- First area he focuses on
	- Communication is #1: takes practice and the sooner you start the better
		- Verbal and written skills
	- Teamwork: Practice it during academia
	- Negotiation: there are online courses you can take. Its not about manipulation but understanding other's motivations
	- Time management: priority management
♫ Business Skills
	- Understand the context of their work
		- Goals of org.
	- Pick an industry and company that you resonate with
	- Keep up with trends
	- Financial literacy - be able to understand numbers
	- Make a connection between your work and finances
♫ Hard Skills
	- Tech moves so fast so continuously learning is important
	- 

♫ Cultural Fit
	- Culture is the aggregate of everyone's behavior
	- Almost impossible to understand culture before you join the company
	- Employer is looking for someone who is already thinking of it
	- Look @ their website




Look at finances / strategy of a company and connect it to your work during an interview
